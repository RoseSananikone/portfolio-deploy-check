I went to the Us By Night event in Antwerp on the 25th of September 2026 and attended 2 Adobe talks which were very interesting. One was about branding for a jewelry company and using AI and Firefly to help with the process. The other one was about After Effects and some hidden features that can save time or create cool effects. The speaker also showed some Beta features that were in the works and they looked really interesting! From this creative festival, there were 2 companies that I vividly remember and interested me.

The first is [Rebel9](https://www.rebel9.co.kr/en/), they had an interactive installation where you could spray paint a wall with a spray bottle that they built. It was basically an interactive graffiti LED wall. I thought it was cool so I asked a few questions on how they made this project, I had a vague idea of how they made it and how it worked but I got a bit more details. For example, there were 2 sensor cameras at the corner of the top wall of the "canvas" and each spray can inside had a chip/sensor that would be activated when someone would press down on it and also a motion sensor inside so you could change the colour and all the coding was made in unity too.

The second is [Form Play](https://formplay.co) which was an animation studio. They had a talk about their projects and how they animated things that aren't usually very human like shapes. It was a clip on how 1 shape, a rectangle, could still have so much personality and life to it through animation and small micro-animations. It was very interesting and impressive to look at the different examples of their work.
