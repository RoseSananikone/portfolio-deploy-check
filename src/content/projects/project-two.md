Fact Check is an interactive storytelling website designed as a scrolling one-pager. Visitors are guided through the arena by a mysterious, powerful narrator who sets the record straight: gladiators rarely fought to the death. They were valuable entertainment, and Rome couldn't afford to lose them. As you scroll, the narrator slowly reveals more of himself, until the final section shows who he really is, the spirit of a deceased Roman emperor.

## The Task

In an age of fake news and half-truths, the brief asked us to pick a widespread historical misconception and use it as the starting point for a story. The goal was to create a storytelling experience that intrigues, informs, immerses and inspires visitors to think critically about fact versus fiction.

The project was a design assignment, so no code was involved. The final result is a clickable prototype in Figma, including interactions and animation states.

## The Misconception

**The myth:** Gladiators fought until one of them died.
**The truth:** Most fights did not end in death. Gladiators were expensive to train and maintain, so their owners had every reason to keep them alive. Fights were closer to a modern boxing match or wrestling show. Some were even predetermined, and it was the gladiators' job to make it look spectacular.

## Concept: The Narrator

I wanted the visitor to experience the story the way a Roman crowd might have: entertained, and a little manipulated. That's why the story is told by a narrator who knows far more than he lets on. His voice is confident and authorative, and the visitor gradually realizes that the person telling the story is the one who once decided over life and death in the arena, the emperor himself.

This twist ties into the core message: always ask *who* is telling you the story, and why.

**Target audience:**
Archetype = Rulers
Vibe = Spiritual/ethereal
Tone = Confident, authoritative
